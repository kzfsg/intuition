# Intuition — Promo Video Storyboard (v1)

**Total runtime:** ~70s · **Format:** 16:9 hero cut (note alt 9:16 crops per scene) · **Tone:** relatable dread → calm relief → confident momentum

A classic problem → solution → proof → CTA arc. Voiceover (VO) is kept close to the source script; lines are tightened for breath and a few words trimmed for delivery.

---

## ACT 1 — THE HOOK (0:00–0:10)

### Scene 1 · The Wall of Code — `0:00–0:05`
- **Visual:** Full-screen Claude Code terminal, mid-stream. Dense output flying past — abstractions, generics, a `useReducer` + custom hook + zod schema combo. Text scrolls slightly too fast to read. Cold blue/gray light on kzf's face (off-screen glow).
- **On-screen text:** none (let the chaos breathe)
- **VO:** *"Have you ever been three prompts in… and realised you understand nothing Claude is saying?"*
- **Notes:** Sound design carries this — rapid keystroke clatter, a rising synth tension. Subtle screen-shake/zoom-in on the code to feel overwhelm. **9:16:** crop tight on the scrolling code column.

### Scene 2 · The Turn — `0:05–0:10`
- **Visual:** Hard cut. Camera pulls back / whip-pan off the screen to **kzf's face**, lit warmer now. A beat of eye contact. Slight wry smile.
- **On-screen text:** none
- **VO:** *"We've been there. And here's the solution."*
- **Notes:** This is the emotional pivot — let the music drop out for a half-second of silence before "And here's the solution." That silence is the gear-change.

---

## ACT 2 — THE REVEAL (0:10–0:25)

### Scene 3 · Logo + Tagline — `0:10–0:15`
- **Visual:** Clean cut to brand. Intuition wordmark animates in on the dark theme. Soft glow, no clutter.
- **On-screen text:** **Intuition** — *an IDE that teaches you at the pace you learn.*
- **VO:** *"Intuition — an IDE that teaches you at the same pace you learn."*
- **Notes:** First time we see the product brand. Let it sit. Confident, quiet.

### Scene 4 · Indexing — `0:15–0:25`
- **Visual:** The real interface. A codebase opens; an indexing progress indicator runs. Files light up node-by-node as they're understood — imply the knowledge graph forming (subtle connecting lines between files/functions).
- **On-screen text:** progress UI → **✓ Index Complete**
- **VO:** *"Intuition indexes your codebase and gives you lessons — live teaching for every function. Student, entry-level, or senior: you pick the depth."*
- **Notes:** The "✓ Index Complete" moment should land like a satisfying *thunk* — payoff after the chaos of Scene 1. Show the difficulty selector (Student / Entry / Senior) as a visible toggle.

---

## ACT 3 — THE PROOF (0:25–0:55)

### Scene 5 · For the Vibe Coder — `0:25–0:40`
- **Visual:** Split focus. Left: generated code the user didn't write. Right: an Intuition lesson panel slides in — a mini "React fundamentals" lesson tied to *that exact code* (e.g. "What `useState` is doing here"). Highlight links code line → concept.
- **On-screen text:** *Pure vibe coder? Perfect.*
- **VO:** *"A complete vibe coder? That's fine. Intuition learns from you and teaches the foundations of every framework — right where you're using them."*
- **Notes:** Show the lesson being *contextual*, not a generic tutorial. The hook is "it teaches the code in front of you."

### Scene 6 · The Living Codebase — `0:40–0:55`
- **Visual:** A function gets edited / refactored. The associated lesson **updates in real time** — old explanation crossfades to new. Pull out to show the knowledge graph re-wiring around the change, only the touched nodes re-indexing (visualize "minimal indexing cost" — most of the graph stays static, a small cluster pulses).
- **On-screen text:** *Lessons that update with your code.* · small tag: *powered by open knowledge graphs*
- **VO:** *"Constantly changing codebase? Your lessons update in real time. We use an open-source second brain and knowledge graphs to track changes — with minimal indexing cost."*
- **Notes:** This is your technical differentiator — make the "only the changed part re-indexes" visual unmistakable. That's the moment a technical viewer leans in.

---

## ACT 4 — CLOSE (0:55–1:10)

### Scene 7 · Montage + Promise — `0:55–1:05`
- **Visual:** Fast, confident montage — difficulty toggle flipping, a lesson completing, the graph glowing whole, kzf nodding at the screen (callback to Scene 2, now relaxed). Warm light throughout.
- **On-screen text:** *Understand everything you build.*
- **VO:** *"Stop shipping code you don't understand. Start building intuition."*
- **Notes:** Mirror Scene 1's framing but inverted — calm, in control. Music resolves.

### Scene 8 · Logo + CTA — `1:05–1:10`
- **Visual:** Intuition wordmark, centered, dark theme. URL / download CTA.
- **On-screen text:** **Intuition** · *Download / [yoursite]*
- **VO:** *(optional)* *"Intuition. The IDE that teaches."*
- **Notes:** Hold 2s. End card.

## Production notes & open decisions

1. **Runtime ~70s.** For a 30s cut (TikTok/X/ads), drop Scene 5 *or* 6 and tighten Act 1 to one scene.
2. **kzf on-camera** appears in Scenes 2 and 7 only (bookends) — cheap to shoot, high emotional payoff. Alternative: fully screen-recording + VO with no face.
3. **The knowledge-graph visual** (Scenes 4 & 6) is the hardest to produce but the strongest differentiator. Confirm whether the real UI can show this today or it needs a motion-graphics overlay.
4. **VO tweaks:** "every single function" → "every function"; run-on lines split for breath. Original meaning preserved.
